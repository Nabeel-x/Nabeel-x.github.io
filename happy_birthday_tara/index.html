For Tara

A little something, across every mile

Happy Birthday, Tara

There are miles between us, and I won’t pretend that doesn’t hurt. Some nights the distance feels heavier than I know how to say.

All I want is to be close to you: to hear your laugh in the same room, to hold your hand, to share the small, ordinary moments with you today. Birthdays are meant to be held close, and I wish I could be right there beside you.

But here is what I know for sure: the distance has never made me doubt us, not once. Every mile only shows me how far I would go to be with you.

I’m with you forever, Tara.

Not just today. In every sunrise, every countdown to the next hello, until the day we never have to say goodbye again.

Happy Birthday, my love. 🎂

Forever yours,
Nabeel
